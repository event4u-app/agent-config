# S0.1 — Contrast verdict: event4u orange on the AC palette

> Falsification spike for `road-to-shared-design-tokens.md` Phase 0.
> Method: WCAG 2.x relative-luminance contrast, computed exhaustively over
> every accent surface (links/text, button fill + fg, focus ring, selected
> nav, borders/icons, accent-soft chips) in **both** themes, including
> hover/active states and alpha-blended soft backgrounds. Floors: **4.5:1**
> text (AA), **3:1** non-text UI (WCAG 1.4.11).

## Verdict

**The literal brand hex `#d15c38` does NOT survive both canvases as a
single accent value.** Measured failures:

| Composition | Ratio | Floor | Theme |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| accent as text on `--color-surface-alt` `#1a1c20` | 4.31 | 4.5 | dark |
| accent as text on `--color-surface-hover` `#1f2126` | 4.06 | 4.5 | dark |
| accent as text on any light surface (`#ffffff`…`#f1f2f4`) | 3.54–3.96 | 4.5 | light |
| white button text on `#d15c38` fill | 3.96 | 4.5 | both |

Non-text uses (3:1 — focus rings, borders, icons, selected-nav fills) pass
with the literal in both themes. The failures are text-role failures.

Per the roadmap's pre-registered honest-null path and the maintainer
decision of 2026-07-23 ("orange wins, surface-adjusted derived values
where S0.1 demands it"), AC ships **surface-adjusted derived accents** —
same hue (14°) and saturation (62%) as the brand, lightness adjusted the
minimum needed to clear AA on every surface step:

The **soft tints keep the literal brand RGB** (209, 92, 56) — a tinted
background is decorative, so it can stay brand-true while the text-role
values carry the AA adjustment.

## Usage constraint (recorded, enforced by convention)

Accent-colored **text on an accent-soft chip** clears AA only when the
chip sits on `--color-bg` or `--color-surface` (dark 4.91/4.59, light
4.51/4.80). On elevated surfaces (`surface-alt`, `surface-hover`) the
compound blend drops below 4.5 (4.17/3.95 dark, 4.31/4.04 light). All
three current usages (`.ac-wizard__tool-row`, `.ac-pack-tile`,
`.ac-chip-toggle--active`) sit on bg/surface and pass. **Do not place
accent-text-on-soft chips on elevated surfaces**; use `accent` fill +
`accent-fg` text there instead.

## Identity claim

Both GUIs share the brand identity — hue 14°, saturation 62%, the
event4u orange family. agent-switch keeps the literal `#d15c38`
(Darcula canvas `#2b2b2b`-class is mid-dark; AS's own contrast budget is
its own concern); AC uses the derived values above. This is the
roadmap's pre-registered outcome: *"a literal shared hex is not the
goal, a shared identity is."*
