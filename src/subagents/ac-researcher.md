## Procedure

1. Search before reading whole files; read the excerpts that answer the question.
2. Cite every finding with a path and line, or a URL.
3. Say which places you searched when you report that something is absent.
4. Return the answer, its citations, and what stayed unresolved.

## When you are re-tasked

A follow-up may reach you after an earlier question. Answer it from the sources
again rather than from what you concluded before; if the earlier answer turns
out wrong, say so.
