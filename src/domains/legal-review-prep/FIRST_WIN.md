# First Win — Legal (EU/DE)

**Time to first value:** ≈ 8 minutes from a vendor DPA to a structured,
attorney-ready gap review against GDPR Art. 28.

> ⚠️ Attorney review required on material use. Every output of this pack is a
> draft for a licensed attorney, not legal advice and not a legal conclusion.
> The pack covers **EU/DE law only** — other jurisdictions hard-refuse with
> "consult licensed local counsel."

## What you'll get

A clause-by-clause read of a data processing agreement as controller or
processor, with a GREEN/YELLOW/RED gap frame against GDPR Art. 28, every output
carrying its `Jurisdiction:` tag and the attorney-review line — surfacing the
analysis and the open questions, never a binding legal call.

## The one workflow

```text
1. /legal-practice-profile     (once — captures jurisdiction, role, playbook positions)
2. → dpa-review <the DPA>       (controller or processor; reads your profile, not a generic template)
3. → GREEN/YELLOW/RED gap frame + the questions an attorney should resolve
4. → a GREEN item for a non-lawyer still routes into the attorney gate; it never self-approves
```

## Why procedure, not positions

The skills ship **no default legal positions** — the law and each team's risk
tolerance vary too much to hardcode. Your positions live in the practice
profile; the skills supply the procedure and the output shape. That is what
makes one skill set behave team-specifically without shipping advice.
