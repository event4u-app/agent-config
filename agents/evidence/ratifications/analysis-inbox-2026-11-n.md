## Gain, risk, rollback

- **Gain:** the eight roadmaps' fixes ship together — trust-boundary repairs
  (forwarded-message merge grant, `settings.local.json`, segment-wise path
  containment, workflow template injection), records that report what they
  dropped, CI greens that saw what they certify, unsourced statutory figures
  removed, host-name collisions removed.
- **Risk:** the bundle ceiling is 3,200 bytes (0.2 %) looser; a later addition
  of that size passes without a raise. The new owner-question-id gate in
  `consistency.yml` could block a merge on a false positive.
- **Rollback:** revert the merge commit of the round's PR; the ceiling and the
  gate return with it. The gate alone can be dropped by reverting its step.
