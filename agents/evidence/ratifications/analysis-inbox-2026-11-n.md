- **Gain:** the eight roadmaps' fixes ship together — trust-boundary repairs
  (forwarded-message merge grant, `settings.local.json`, segment-wise path
  containment, workflow template injection), records that report what they
  dropped, CI greens that saw what they certify, unsourced statutory figures
  removed, host-name collisions removed.
- **Risk:** the new owner-question-id gate in `consistency.yml` could block a
  merge on a false positive.
- **Rollback:** revert the merge commit of the round's PR; the gate alone can be
  dropped by reverting its step.
