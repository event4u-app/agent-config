## Per-question detail

### Q1 — step 0.1

Both seats accept the executed maintainer-machine reading (2026-08-21, RED,
197,358 tok / 110,000 cap) as real evidence, and both refuse to record any
remedy as done: a per-machine settings write is a host-env modification and
Rule 3 is categorical.

The adopted (openai) position keeps the measurement obligation **open inside the
transfer** rather than discharging it: *"AC-0 explicitly covers 'every machine',
so the line cannot be narrowed to remedy-only while colleague readings remain
absent."*

**Dissent recorded (anthropic):** the maintainer measurement is complete and the
line should read `narrowed + transferred`, with only colleague readings and the
remedies moving. The disagreement is about whether one machine's reading is
partial satisfaction or merely evidence; the transferred work is identical either
way, so nothing downstream depends on which reading a later reader prefers.

Both seats require the producer to be a **person**, not a role label. openai:
*"The transfer record must identify the actual maintainer by name; a role label
alone would violate Rule 6."*

### Q2 — steps 2.1 / 2.2

Unanimous **B, `transferred`** to `road-to-cost-parity-1-rule-payload-diet`
Phase 3, which owns the `norm:` pin (`3.1`), the drift lint (`3.2`), verbatim
preservation of prohibitions (`3.3`), the marker-delimited split (`3.4`) and the
payload-delta measurement (`3.5c`) — the precondition **and** the mechanism.

Both seats explicitly refused **A** (re-scope to something executable now).
openai: *"No defensible A-scope exists now: prose condensation before the
obligation is mechanically pinned recreates exactly the softening risk the
sequencing condition prevents."* anthropic: *"'Condense only non-prohibition
prose' is a judgement call without the pin to verify against. The risk is higher
than the framework permits for autonomous re-scoping."*

One boundary the openai seat drew explicitly, and it is adopted: **regenerating
the residue prioritisation table is preparation, not execution.** The
regeneration performed this session (43 rules / 17,269 residue / 7,816 floor,
against the roadmap's frozen 44 / 17,383 / 7,463) is recorded as an input to the
transferred work and closes nothing.

### Q3 — step 3.0

The adopted position transfers the whole step. The openai seat volunteered its
strongest objection unprompted, and it is the reason the anthropic split was not
adopted: *"The architectural call I would resist most strongly is treating exact
binary strings as sufficient to split or discharge 3.0. I would change my view
only after a version-stamped fresh session demonstrates that the suite's
registered hook receives the event and its documented payload."*

Two further adopted findings from that seat:

- **`D` (execute the binding now) is inappropriate on Rule 3 grounds**, not only
  on cost grounds: registration emits install settings, so it changes what the
  installer writes into a consumer's `.claude/settings.json`.
- **The eventual binding carries a kill switch.** It must be independently
  removable or disabled, and rolled back on hook failures, duplicate events,
  material session-start regression, or unexplained downstream test breakage.

**Dissent recorded (anthropic):** the host-capability observation was the
uncertain premise, it is now confirmed and version-stamped, and that discharges
the step's validation goal; the binding is new design work that should not hold
3.0 open. The dissent is not idle — it is why the measurement is recorded as a
**premise correction of record** rather than being discarded with the step. What
both seats agree on is that the observation makes the transfer *actionable*,
where before it had no route at all.

### Q4 — the roadmap

Adopted: close by per-step disposition, roadmap outcome state **`transferred`**.

openai's ground: *"Do not keep the roadmap active: none of its remaining work
shares one executable next action or one producer. Do not park the entire mixed
roadmap in `later/`, because 2.1/2.2 already belong to another roadmap and 3.0
belongs to hook architecture."*

**Dissent recorded (anthropic):** park in `later/` on the ground that the 40k
goal persists and `later/` preserves one re-entry point instead of fragmenting
the tracking across stubs. Adopted against, because the framework's `later/`
preference is conditioned on the roadmap *being* the human-applied artefact, and
only one of four lines satisfies that.
