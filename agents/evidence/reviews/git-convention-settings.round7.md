| 1 | high | src/domains/git/pr/create/command.md:148 | Under `update_strategy: rebase` the "every subsequent push" flow hits a dead end. The required `0 0` therefore never holds and the rebase never starts. A plain push of the behind branch is refused as stale, and a merge is forbidden under rebase, so neither way forward is open.
