| 1 | medium | src/scripts/sync_pr_branch.ts:797 | The memoized target deps do not reach the merge. `main` reads `git.update_strategy` at the target SHA through `memoTargetDeps`, and the comment on `memoTargetDeps` says it exists so one run cannot "judge the strategy against one commit and sync against another when the target moves in between". But `sync()` counts behind against the freshly fetched tracking refs (`countBehind('HEAD', ref)`, line 744ff), and `integrateWithPinnedBase` pins its merges via a new, un-memoized `makeGitDeps(repo).remoteSha`, which also ignores the injected `deps` parameter. If the target advances between the strategy read and the merge, the run merges a target commit whose carrier was never read. That is the gap the memo claims to close, and tests that inject `deps` cannot see it. | accepted-risk | a race, not a misread: the strategy is read at the target sha resolved once; a target that advances in the seconds between that read and the merge's own last-moment re-pin is merged under the strategy that was in force at read time, and a strategy change landing in exactly that window is the only case that diverges — at worst one merge commit, nothing rewritten. The re-pin before mutating is deliberate (same disposition as round 2 finding 4 and round 8 of the predecessor review). Follow-up candidate: re-read the carrier when the pinned sha differs from the strategy sha. Accepted by: implementing agent, disclosed to the owner in the PR description |
