## What this record is, and what it is not

An owner-directive record makes an owner's order visible in the reviewable
artefact instead of only in a chat transcript.

It **grants nothing**. No gate reads it, no command treats its presence as an
authorization, and an agent cannot mint one to authorize itself: a record whose
verbatim instruction does not appear in the owner's own turn is a fabricated
record, which is a defect, not a weaker form of consent. The authority is the
owner's turn; this file is its receipt. In particular it does not decide the
settings class or the verb — those wait for the ADR of step 0.2.
