## Method

- **Readers, code**: non-test files under `src/` with extension `.ts/.js/.sh/.bash/.py` that either contain the dotted path (`grep -rlF '<dotted.key>' src`) or contain the leaf as an identifier AND the parent segment in the same file (`grep -rlE '\b<leaf>\b'` filtered by `grep -qE '\b<parent>\b'`). Excluded as plumbing (they declare, validate, migrate or display a key, they do not act on it): `src/server/schemas/settings.ts`, `src/ui/wizard/*`, `src/ui/settings/*`, the legacy flat-key mapping table in `src/scripts/install.ts:330-345`, `settingsCarveOut.ts`, `settingsClasses.ts`, `generate_settings_reference.ts`, `sync_agent_settings.ts`, `validate_agent_settings.ts`, `substituteTemplate.ts`, `settings_renamed_keys.ts`, `MERGEABLE_KEYS` in `_lib/agent_settings.ts`, `src/config/*`. Generic leaves (`mode`, `enabled`, `intent`, `grounding`, `orchestrator`, `cadence`) produced noise; those counts were corrected by hand and are marked `(hand)`.
- **Readers, md**: files under `src/` (rules, skills, commands, contexts) containing the dotted path, excluding the reference page `src/agent-src/templates/agent-settings.md`. These are model-carried readers ("Read `x.y` from `.agent-settings.yml`").
- **Verdict rule applied**: `ready` = zero readers, OR the named mechanism exists in the tree AND, once the key is gone, the effective behaviour in the shipped default situation equals the template default (reader fallback == template default, and the mechanism would not flip the outcome). `not-ready` = mechanism missing/partial, or the absent-key fallback differs from the template default, or adopting the mechanism would change the shipped default. `unclear` = a fact I could not settle from the tree.
- A `ready` verdict still owes downstream edits (drop the reader line in the md/code that names the key); it means nothing blocks the deletion on the policy's two conditions.
