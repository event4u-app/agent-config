# Stub: road to a concern effect census

> **Stub — not active work.** From round `inbox-2026-11-hb` (2026-11-20). It
> needs elapsed time, not effort: 30 days of real sessions after
> `road-to-a-hook-system-that-pays-for-what-it-does` ships.

**What.** From the dispatch journal over 30 days: per concern, how often it ran
and how often it produced any effect (deny, ask, injected context, a notice, a
record something later read). Counts only, no content.

**Why.** "Too many concerns" is asserted by the concern-count lint and shown by
nothing. A census turns the question into data: which default-on concerns have
never produced an effect.

**Trigger.** 30 days after the active roadmap above is archived. The report then
goes to the owner, who decides per concern: keep, default-off or remove. No
concern is removed by an agent on this data alone, and no security guard is
removed because it never fired.
