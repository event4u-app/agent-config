# Road to a verdict that shows its frame

> **Source:** `agents/tmp.old/inbox-2026-11-p/` (topic t1: owner transcript, a
> two-revision supplied plan, two further AC drafts, a separate-engine plan, a
> probe script, two sample clips).
> **Arrivals:** 1 — `inbox-2026-11-p` (2026-11-03).

## Goal

The local media path can see an event that lasts four frames, names it by frame
index, and reports what the container says about the file's origin — without a
model, a GPU or a paid provider. Concretely: `media_bundle` keeps sanitised
container tags and reports provenance signals (absence is "no provenance signal
found", never "human-made"); a `--detail forensic` mode samples every frame of a
short clip and skips near-duplicate dropping; anchors carry `fN`; strips and a
labelled grid cut the evidence region; and `video-qa.sh` gives video the same
measured floor `audio-qa.sh` gives audio, consumed by `uiRenderFilm.ts`.

Reproduced at the pin (lane report, scratchpad only): on the 361-frame sample
the balanced plan kept 13 frames and exactly one inside the 17-frame window of
interest; `aigc_info` and `encoder` tags were discarded; frames carry no index;
this host's ffmpeg has no `vidstabdetect` and no `drawtext`.
