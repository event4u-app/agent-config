# banana-arc — golden reference script

> Three-tier golden run for the `/video:from-script` pipeline. Each
> scene exercises a different branch of the adapter contract.

## Scene 01-simple

**Setting.** A quiet wooden writing desk by a window. Soft afternoon light.

**Action.** Banana stands at the desk, looking thoughtfully at the
notebook in left peel. Static camera. No dialogue.

**Beat.** Quiet contemplation.

**Duration.** 4 seconds.

**Camera.** Static, MS framing, subject on left third.

**Lens.** 35mm f/2.0.

**Lighting.** Warm window light from camera-right, soft ambient fill.

**Audio.** None.

**Negatives.** No text overlay, no extra characters, no motion blur.

## Scene 02-dialogue-native-audio

**Setting.** Same desk, same window. Dusk light now.

**Action.** Banana looks up from the notebook, speaks one line. Soft
ambient room tone underneath.

**Beat.** Reluctant courage — the character has decided something.

**Duration.** 6 seconds.

**Camera.** Slow dolly-in, MCU framing.

**Lens.** 50mm f/1.8.

**Lighting.** Warm key from camera-right, deep blue fill from window
behind.

**Audio.**

- Dialogue: "I think it's time to go."
- Ambient: soft room tone, distant clock ticking.

**Negatives.** No text overlay, no extra characters, no soap-opera
lighting.

## Scene 03-edge-duration

**Setting.** A forest path at golden hour. Dappled light through
leaves.

**Action.** Banana walks the path, the camera tracking alongside in a
fast lateral move. Notebook still in left peel.

**Beat.** Forward momentum.

**Duration.** 10 seconds.

**Camera.** Fast pan-left, tracking.

**Lens.** 28mm f/2.8.

**Lighting.** Golden-hour backlight, soft amber rim on character.

**Audio.** None.

**Negatives.** No text overlay, no extra characters, no lens flare, no
fish-eye distortion.
