# ADR evidence sweep, 2026-08 — the whole estate, challenged once

> **Mandate.** Owner instruction, 2026-08-21: ADRs must be fact-based —
> community best practice, measurements, tests — not gut feeling from one or
> more agents; mark origin human vs agentic; grade how easily each decision may
> be overturned; **question ALL of them**; identify which ones block autonomous
> development of consumer projects or of the suite itself.
>
> **Roadmap.** `road-to-evidence-based-adr-governance`, Phase 3.
> **Contract.** `docs/contracts/adr-layout.md` § Provenance and evidence.
> **Doctrine.** ADR-238 (`status: proposed`).
> **Corpus.** 178 flat records in `docs/decisions/` + 7 per-area records in
> `docs/adrs/**` = **185 records** at the head of this branch (177 flat before
> ADR-238 was added by the same change).

## What this artifact is, and what it is not

It **is** a challenge disposition per record: would today's facts justify
accepting this decision exactly as written, if it did not already exist?

It **is not** a classification write. Not one line of ADR frontmatter is
changed by this sweep. That boundary is the council's ruling and it rests on a
distinction worth stating plainly, because it is what makes the owner's "question
ALL of them" satisfiable today: **questioning a record does not require
classifying it.** A record can be challenged and come out as
`INSUFFICIENT-EVIDENCE-TO-CLASSIFY`, which is an honest disposition and not a
deferral. `adr-layout.md:141` permits investigation, proposals and reversible
experiments under the `unclassified` default; it does not override the
bulk-classification clause at `:153`, and a backfill that ran ahead of its
ratification would invert the approval flow into write-and-hope.

It **is not** an authority change. No disposition here lets an agent supersede
anything on its own. The grade prices the reopen *record*; who may reopen stays
with the transition-based discriminator (ADR-238 § 2).

### The independence limit, stated rather than implied

The gold grades in the anchor set below were adjudicated by the same session
that authored the schema and this roadmap. That is **not** the external
adjudication `claim:adr-grade-accuracy-vs-gold` requires, and it cannot be — a
party cannot be independent of a design it wrote. So:

- the anchor set functions as **calibration** for the tranche reviewers, which
  is a real and separate use;
- `claim:adr-grade-accuracy-vs-gold` stays `unbacked`. This sweep does not
  supply its data, and reading it as though it did would be exactly the
  self-review the whole design objects to.

**Two calibration lessons a reviewer must carry into a tranche**, both of them
places where the obvious read is wrong:

1. **A council attribution is not a downgrade to zero — look for what else is
   on the line.** ADR-048 would read E0 to a marker-counting pass and is E1,
   because the same line carries observed counts. Read the line, not the tag.
2. **Evidence a record *cites from elsewhere* is not that record's evidence.**
   ADR-118 leans on ADR-216's capacity finding; that keeps ADR-118's premise
   defensible without giving ADR-118 a grade it did not earn.
3. **A word count is not a provenance signal.** ADR-104 contains 46 occurrences
   of `council` and is a **human** decision: the council is what the record is
   *about* — the config being relocated — not who decided it. Distinguish the
   decision's subject matter from its decision-maker before either axis.

### Correction to this anchor set — ADR-104, found by a reviewer

ADR-104's provenance was adjudicated `agentic` on exactly the reasoning lesson 3
now forbids: 46 occurrences of `council`, read as volume of agreement. The
census pass disagreed and was right. `:16` reads "Maintainer directive
(standing, restated and sharpened)", and a grep for a council *decision* marker
(`council decided|convergence|disposition|verdict|agreed|ruled|two seats|both
seats`) returns **zero** lines. The record is a human directive whose subject
happens to be the council. The E0 grade is unaffected — no measurement is
cited — so only the provenance axis moved.

Two things follow, and the second is the uncomfortable one.

The correction is recorded here rather than quietly applied, because eight
tranche reviewers calibrated against this table and one of its 13 rows was
wrong for the duration. Any tranche row that cites ADR-104 as a
provenance precedent inherits the error.

And it is the exact failure this artifact's own independence limit predicted one
section earlier: the party that wrote the schema adjudicated the anchors, fell
into the trap its own calibration lesson names, and needed an outside pass to
catch it. That is one datum, not a rate — but it is a datum in the direction
`claim:adr-grade-accuracy-vs-gold` exists to measure, and it is why that claim
stays `unbacked` on this sweep's evidence.

## Disposition vocabulary

`KEEP` · `KEEP-BUT-DOWNGRADE` · `AMEND` · `SUPERSEDE (candidate)` ·
`MERGE-INTO-POLICY` · `CONVERT-TO-MEASUREMENT` · `REVIEW-NOW` ·
`HISTORICAL-ONLY` · `INSUFFICIENT-EVIDENCE-TO-CLASSIFY`

`accepted` never wins by default. No record is overturned merely for being
agentic, and none is preserved merely because the corpus is large.

## Per-record protocol — twelve questions, in order

(1) effective decision today; (2) who actually decided; (3) load-bearing
claims; (4) which are facts, which assumptions, which preferences; (5) current
evidence per fact; (6) is that evidence still valid; (7) which alternative was
excluded and why; (8) what it blocks today; (9) is that blocking still
proportional; (10) would the suite accept this decision again on today's facts;
(11) disposition; (12) if blocking, the minimum sufficient authority for the
change.

`Blocking cost` is structured sourced observations or `unknown` — never a
scalar. Interruptions, tokens, blocked items and unavailable capabilities are
not commensurable, and one ordinal collapses distinct failure modes into a
number that cannot guide action.

## Central adjudication

One normalisation pass over all 185 records after the tranches, which the
tranches cannot do for themselves: a cross-record citation only coheres if both
grades are adjudicated together, and tranche-local drift is invisible from
inside a tranche.

### Coverage

185 records, all covered: 13 adjudicated as anchors up front, 165 further flat
records and 7 per-area records across eight tranches. No record was skipped and
none was sampled — the owner's instruction was to question all of them, and the
count is the check on that.

### Blinded overlap — the disagreement, published rather than smoothed

17 records were assigned to a second reviewer who was not told it was a double.
**16 came back doubled** (one reviewer covered its primary list only, so that
record has a single grade — stated because a 17/17 claim would be false).

Of the 16: **provenance agreed on 14, strength agreed on 14.** Three records
disagreed, one of them on both axes. Adjudicated here, with the reasoning, so
the resolution is auditable rather than asserted:

| Record | Reviewer A | Reviewer B | Adjudicated | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ADR-009 | `mixed` / E1 | `human` / E0 | **`unknown` / E0** | The record carries no decision-maker marker at all — no council attribution, no "maintainer", no measurement. | ADR-037 | E1 | E2 | **`agentic` / E1** | `:23` names the AI council as decider and `:69` records its unanimity — and unanimity is not evidence. | ADR-085 | `mixed` | `human` | **`mixed`** | `:25` "explicit maintainer decision (the demand trigger)" and `:56-58` the verbatim owner instruction, with a two-round peer-reviewed council session choosing the mechanism. Human premise, agent mechanism — the textbook `mixed` case. |

**What this measures, and what it does not.** 14/16 on each axis is an
inter-reviewer agreement rate. It is **not** accuracy: reviewers who searched the
same way against the same rubric can agree and both be wrong, and this
repository has the receipt — 44 engine-shaped REJECTs accumulated under
correlated council agreement and were disposed of by one measurement. So this
number goes to `claim:adr-grade-accuracy-vs-gold` as an input, not as a result;
that claim needs an externally adjudicated gold sample and stays `unbacked`.

One datum points the same way. The anchor set itself carried a wrong provenance
on ADR-104 for the duration of the sweep, adjudicated by the party that wrote
the schema, and an outside pass caught it. That is one error in 13 anchors, which
is not a rate — but it is a measured instance of the failure mode this design's
independence limit predicts.

### The heuristic's blind spot, measured

`evidence_census.ts` proposed **E4 = 0** across all 185 records, and declared in
its own header why: a heuristic cannot establish that a real consumer depends on
an interface or that a legal obligation applies, so it never proposes E4.

Reviewers found **3**. The clearest is ADR-205, whose basis is a court ruling on
hotlink IP transmission — an external legal constraint applied to the decision it
supports, which is the definition of E4 and is invisible to any marker scan.

That is the census working as documented rather than failing: a stated blind
spot, now measured at 3 records instead of asserted. It is also the concrete
argument for keeping the census proposal-only — a pass that wrote its own
grades would have written `E0`/`E1` over three E4 records and nobody would have
looked again.

the natural probe and it produced the wrong answer; recorded because the next
person will reach for the same grep.
