# RDP L6 orchestrator-isolation eval — first run (2026-06-17)

Settles the **measurement** half of Phase-1 step 3 of
`road-to-rdp-eval-and-promotion`: does the `reasoning-orchestrator` (the RDP
disciplines run as one **ordered chain**) gain over **distributed-only** (the
same disciplines as an unordered buffet)? The keep/revert **decision** is routed
to the maintainer / follow-up per the council (below) — it is not settled here.

## Method

`run_quality_eval.py --mode l6`. Same controlled differential, the orchestrator
is the only variable:

- `distributed` = `BASE + RDP_BLOCK` (disciplines listed, no coordination)
- `orchestrated` = `+ ORCHESTRATOR_PREAMBLE` (run them as one ordered chain with
  handoffs; do not force the chain on trivial tasks)

5 slots (01 false-positive check + 04/05/06/07 orchestrator-relevant), standard
band → `claude-haiku-4-5`, strong band → `claude-sonnet-4-5`. Single rater.
Spend $0.043.

## The finding the pre-registered condition could not see

Both flip-condition legs fire (gain <10% AND FP >15%), which reads as a clean
**revert**. But the eval revealed the condition is **mis-specified**: it assumed
"orchestrator" is a single intervention. The data shows **two separable
mechanisms**:

1. **Multi-stage tool coherence** (slot 06, strong host): the ordered chain
   prevented a real failure — distributed-only drifted into tool-call exploration
   and guessed "build a template"; orchestrated stayed coherent and stopped
   cleanly, with **46% fewer tokens**. A genuine win.
2. **Stateless single-turn reasoning** (slot 07): the chain caused
   over-production — it elaborated a worked example where distributed-only
   correctly asked one question and stopped. A genuine loss.

Applying a univariate threshold to this multivariate result violates the
epistemic contract of pre-registration. N=5 is also far too small to
distinguish a 20% FP rate from 5% or 35%.

## Council verdict (claude-sonnet-4-5 + gpt-4o, peer-review, 2026-06-17, $0.10)

- **L6 — do NOT autonomously keep or revert.** The pre-registered condition is
  invalidated by the two-mechanism finding; `keep-but-narrow` is not
  implementable without a runtime gate this no-runtime package does not have.
  Record the split finding; the keep/revert decision + a larger-N re-run move to
  the follow-up roadmap (the decision was always the maintainer's).
- **L12 verifier gate — keep** (from the quality run: caught a data-loss
  migration + a blind over-production; calibrated by error-catch rate).
- **L7 — keep tier-2 auto, do NOT promote.** Zero-refusal condition met, but the
  load-bearing evidence is single-rater + ceiling-caveated — insufficient to grow
  the always-on kernel. notes-first already runs as tier-2 in production, so this
  is a no-op decision (no new soak).
- **Phase 3 — spawn a follow-up roadmap** for the soak-gated kernel
  de-prescription + the eval-dependent strong-band recheck; do NOT autonomously
  sweep batch R ("reviewed, not a sweep" is not autonomously evaluable).
- **Biggest risk:** treating thin/multivariate eval data as if it settles an
  architectural decision. Mitigated here by recording findings and deferring the
  keep/revert + kernel edits, not forcing them.
