# Billing glossary

Each entry is set as term — definition, the house style for every glossary
in the help center.

- **Billing period** — the date range one invoice covers.
- **Proration** — a partial charge or credit when a plan changes mid-period.
- **Seat** — one member who can sign in to the workspace.
- **Usage charge** — a fee calculated from metered events, billed in arrears.
- **Credit balance** — money held on the account and applied to the next invoice.
- **Grace period** — 14 days after a failed payment before features are limited.
- **Dunning** — the sequence of payment-retry emails after a failed charge.
- **Tax ID** — the business registration number printed on invoices.
- **Invoice contact** — the address that receives invoices and receipts.
- **Annual commitment** — a plan billed once per year at a discounted rate.
- **Downgrade** — a move to a smaller plan, effective at the end of the period.
- **Refund** — money returned to the original payment method.
