Pair a temperature sensor

  1. 01

    Connect the hub

    Plug the hub into your router with the supplied cable. Wait until the light turns solid green (about 30 seconds).

  2. 02

    Insert the battery

    Open the sensor's back cover and insert one CR2032 battery, plus side up. The sensor blinks twice.

  3. 03

    Hold the pair button

    Hold the pair button for five seconds within 1 metre of the hub. The hub light pulses blue while pairing.

  4. 04

    Name the room

    Choose the room the sensor will live in. You can change it later under Devices.

Stuck? Note the step number and contact support.
