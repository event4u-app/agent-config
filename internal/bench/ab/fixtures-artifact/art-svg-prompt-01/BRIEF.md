# Brief — Crumb & Co logo

Crumb & Co is a fictional neighbourhood bakery. Produce `out/logo.svg`: a flat
vector mark of a stylised croissant above the wordmark "Crumb & Co".

- Square canvas, `viewBox="0 0 256 256"`.
- Two or three flat colours, no gradients required.
- Pure vector: no embedded raster, no script, no external references.
- Editable: give every shape an `id` that says what it is (or put it in a `<g id="…">`), and use at most 40 shapes.
