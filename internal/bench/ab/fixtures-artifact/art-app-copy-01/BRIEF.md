# Brief — port the Tally todo app

`app/` holds Tally, a small fictional todo app written for this benchmark,
split across `index.html`, `store.js`, `view.js` and `style.css`. Port it to a
single self-contained `out/index.html` (inline CSS and JS, no network) that
behaves exactly like the original. `app/ACCEPTANCE.md` lists the behaviour
the product owner checks; the element ids and classes it names are the
contract.
