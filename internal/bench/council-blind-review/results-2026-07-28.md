# Results — council blind-review A/B (2026-07-28)

## Setup

- 10 already-decided council questions, 2 arms each (20 `council:run`
  invocations, members claude-sonnet-4-5 + gpt-4o, same budgets both arms).
  Measured spend: **$1.58 total** (0 failed runs).
- Synthesis per arm: fresh isolated sonnet session, artifact-only input
  (arm a attributed, arm b blind A–E + required fields; de-anonymization
  map stripped from the synthesizer's copy, kept in the archive artifact).
- Degradation/verdict evaluation: independent sonnet evaluator over all 10
  pairs (artifact + both syntheses per question), criteria verbatim from
  the pre-registration.

## Pre-registered rule outcomes

- **Ü1 (blind synthesis): ADOPTED.** 0/10 crit-a (blind synthesis never
  contradicted a unanimous member position) and 0/10 crit-b (never cited
  evidence absent from all member responses) — the council-tightened
  "zero degradation triggers over ≥10 pairs" rule is satisfied; the
  literature prior (Panickssery NeurIPS 2024, Koo 2024) carries the rest.
  **Landed:** `blind_chairman` defaults to `true` on `council:run`;
  per-invocation opt-out `--no-blind-chairman`; audit artifact always
  keeps the post-verdict de-anonymization map. Default pinned by test.
- **Ü3 (chairman fields): PENDING maintainer blind rating.** Field present
  + specific in 10/10 arm-b syntheses (evaluator), but the tightened rule
  requires the maintainer — blind — to rate it *decision-influencing* in
  ≥2 of 3 sampled runs. Packet: `blind-rating-packet.md` (R2 column).
- **Ü2 (stances): PENDING maintainer blind rating.** Arms differ
  substantively in 9/10 pairs; the rule requires maintainer blind
  preference for the stance arm in the MAJORITY of those 9. Packet: R1
  column. Until rated, `--stances` stays default-off.

## Honest limitations

- **Verdict drift ≠ attributable treatment effect.** 9/10 pairs changed
  verdict at least partially, but arm b bundles blinding+stances+fields AND
  a fresh synthesizer sample — with no same-arm repeat baseline, part of
  the drift is plausibly sampling variance. The Ü1 adoption does NOT rest
  on drift (its criteria are degradation-shaped); the Ü2 rating should
  read the drift with this caveat in mind.
- 1-person maintainer blind; n=10; single day; `run`-path only.
